# Do university papers repeat old questions? (checked 20 November 2026)

## When SEP started

Karnataka dropped the 4-year NEP degree for the 3-year degree from 2024-25 (government order of
8 May 2024, on the SEP Commission's interim report). The first SEP batch wrote the 3rd-semester
exam in December 2025 / January 2026, so that is the only SEP 3rd-semester paper per subject.
Before that, B.Com ran under NEP from 2021-22.

Davangere NEP B.Com 3rd semester (scheme from 2021-22): Corporate Accounting (B.Com.3.1),
Business Statistics (3.2), Cost Accounting (3.3), Artificial Intelligence (SEC), Advertising /
Entrepreneurial Skills (OEC). Each paper 60 marks SEE + 40 CIE. So only Corporate Accounting and
Business Statistics carry over to SEP; HRD, Accounting Standards and Personality Development are new.

No Davangere B.Com or BBA 3rd-semester NEP papers were found online (karnatakastudy.com has only
five Davangere 1st/2nd-semester papers from 2022, and no BBA HRM papers; other hits were not
Davangere sources).

Compared with BBA Q.P. 36220 (3rd sem, NEP, Jan/Feb 2024), BBA Q.P. 56421 (4th sem, May/June 2026)
and six old B.Com 5th-semester Fundamentals of Statistics papers (2014-2020).

**Result:** 2 of the 13 long questions were copied exactly (same wording and same numbers) from the
Jan/Feb 2024 NEP BBA paper, and most of the rest repeat old question types with new figures.

**Caveat:** BBA Q.P. 36220 does not print a university name (neither does the Dec 2025 paper); its
layout (Q.P. Code top right, NEP Scheme, 60 marks, 2 hours) matches Davangere's NEP papers, and
Abuzar collected it with his own papers, but it is not proven to be Davangere's. If it is, the
setter reused an older paper of the same university; if not, the setter copied from another
university's paper. Either way, exact repeats from older papers happen, and BBA papers of the same
subject are worth collecting alongside B.Com ones.

## College model exams against the Dec 2025 paper

Exact questions in both (from the theory-bank tags): HRD 1 of about 48, Personality Development 1
of about 29, Accounting Standards 2 of about 27. So the wording is new, **but the topics mostly
repeat**. In HRD, the model exam asked again about subsystems of HRD, learning principles, HRD
mechanisms and climate, factors in employee behaviour, outbound training, HR/HRD audit, aptitude,
developing human capacity, training techniques, recent trends and competency mapping, and the
definition of HRD: same topics, new wording. Topic overlap is the strongest prediction signal; it is
what `papers/human-resource-development-predicted-questions/` ranks on.

## What would make prediction stronger

Old Davangere papers (NEP 2023-2025, B.Com and BBA, 3rd semester, and any May/June 2026
supplementary papers) for Corporate Accounting and Business Statistics; for HRD, Accounting
Standards and Personality Development, any older Davangere paper with the same subject in another
course (BBA HRM papers, for example).
