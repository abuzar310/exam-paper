## Definitions

- **Statistics** (Croxton and Cowden): "Statistics may be defined as the collection, presentation, analysis and interpretation of numerical data." Statistics is a branch of applied mathematics that deals with the collection, description, analysis and inference of conclusions from quantitative data.
- **Tabulation:** a logical and systematic presentation of statistical data in rows and columns. Rows are horizontal and columns vertical arrangements of data. It gives the data an orderly form and highlights their basic characteristics.
- **Primary data:** data originally collected by an investigator or agency for the first time for a statistical investigation.
- **Secondary data:** data already collected by someone else and used by the investigator.

## Methods of collecting primary data (this book lists five)

1. Direct personal investigation
2. Indirect oral investigation
3. Information received through local correspondents
4. Mailed questionnaire method
5. Schedules sent through enumerators

The teacher's Module 4 (`module-4-introduction-to-statistics.md`) lists six, adding the interview schedule and telephone methods. Answers follow Module 4.
