Dear Sir, 
Sub: Complaint regarding damaged books received. 
 
We regret to inform you that the consignment of books sent against our purchase order 
dated 24 July 2025 was received in a damaged condition. The parcel contained: 
 
1. An Anthology of English Prose – 10 copies 
2. An Anthology of Modern Poetry – 15 copies 
3. Communicative English Textbook – 20 copies 
 
Out of these, 12 copies were found damaged, with torn pages, bent covers, and 
moisture-affected binding. The package also appeared mishandled, indicating poor 
packing during dispatch. 
 
Since these books are meant for student distribution, we cannot issue them in their 
current condition. 
 
We request you too kindly: 
1. Replace the damaged copies at the earliest, 
2. Ensure better packaging in future dispatches, and 
3. Arrange for the return pickup of the defective books, if required. 
 
We expect the replacement stock within 7 working days, as our academic schedule is 
time-bound. We hope you will take prompt action to resolve this issue 
 
Thank you. 
 
Yours faithfully,
