# AS 29 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets

Accounting Standard 29: Provisions, Contingent Liabilities & Contingent Assets.

Complete technical theory, recognition thresholds, measurement principles, and ICAI disclosure frameworks.

## Standard Objective & Scope Framework

AS 29 (Issued 2003)

### Core Objective

Ensures appropriate recognition criteria and measurement bases are applied to provisions, contingent liabilities, and contingent assets, supported by clear note disclosures.

### Scope Exclusions

- Financial instruments carried at fair value.
- Executory contracts (unless onerous).
- Insurance contracts with policyholders.
- Items covered by other AS (AS 7, AS 15, AS 19, AS 22).

## Fundamental Technical Definitions

### Provision

A liability which can be measured only by using a substantial degree of estimation.

### Liability

A present obligation arising from past events, settling which requires an outflow of economic benefits.

### Obligating Event

An event that creates a legal or constructive obligation leaving no realistic alternative to settlement.

## Recognition Criteria for Provisions

All three conditions must be satisfied simultaneously for recognition.

### Condition 1 — Present Obligation

Result of a past obligating event existing at the balance sheet date.

### Condition 2 — Probable Outflow

Outflow of economic resources is more likely than not (> 50% probability).

### Condition 3 — Reliable Estimate

Amount of obligation can be estimated reliably using standard methods.

## Contingent Liabilities: Definition & Accounting

### Two Definitions

- **Possible obligation:** Existence confirmed only by uncertain future events outside entity control.
- **Unrecognized present obligation:** Not recognized because outflow is not probable OR reliable estimate is impossible.

### Accounting Treatment

- **Never Recognized:** Not recorded in financial statements.
- **Note Disclosure:** Disclosed in notes unless outflow possibility is remote.
- **Continuous Review:** Re-assessed each period for status changes.

## Contingent Assets: Principles & Exception

### General Rule (Prudence)

Contingent assets are neither recognized nor disclosed in financial statements to prevent recognizing unrealized income.

### Board's Report Disclosure

If inflow of economic benefit is probable, it is disclosed in the Report of the Approving Authority (Board's Report).

### Virtual Certainty Rule

When inflow becomes virtually certain, the asset and related income are recognized in financial statements.

## Measurement Rules & Key Principles

### Best Estimate

Amount recognized must be the best estimate of expenditure required to settle the present obligation at balance sheet date.

### No Discounting

Under AS 29, provisions are not discounted to present value (unlike Ind AS 37), except where specifically mandated.

### Risks & Uncertainties

Risks and uncertainties must be factored in; however, uncertainty does not justify creating excessive provisions.

## Reimbursements & Specific Restrictions

### Reimbursements

- Recognized only when virtually certain that reimbursement will be received.
- Treated as a separate asset, capped at the provision amount.
- In P&L, provision expense may be presented net of reimbursement.

### Future Operating Losses

- Provisions shall NOT be recognized for future operating losses.
- Future operating losses do not meet definition of a liability or past obligating event.
- Indicates potential impairment of assets under AS 28.

## Onerous Contracts & Restructuring Provisions

### Onerous Contract

Contract where unavoidable costs of fulfilling obligation exceed expected economic benefits.

**Provision Amount:** Lower of cost of fulfilling contract OR penalties from failure to fulfill.

### Restructuring Provisions

Recognized only when detailed formal plan exists and valid expectation is raised before balance sheet date.

**Excludes:** Retraining staff, marketing, or investment in new systems.

## Decision Matrix: Provisions vs Contingencies

| Parameter | Provision | Contingent Liability | Contingent Asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Probability | Probable (> 50%) | Possible (< 50%) | Probable / Uncertain |
| Financial Treatment | Recognized in B/S | Not Recognized | Not Recognized |
| Disclosure Location | Financial Statements | Notes to Accounts | Board's Report |
| If Remote / Certain | N/A | No disclosure if remote | Recognized if virtually certain |

## Mandatory ICAI Disclosure Requirements

### For Each Class of Provision

- Opening and closing carrying amounts.
- Additional provisions made & amounts used/reversed.
- Brief description of obligation nature & timing.

### Prejudicial Exemption Clause

In extremely rare cases where disclosure can seriously prejudice entity position in disputes:

Full disclosures not required; must disclose general nature of dispute & reason for non-disclosure.

## AS 29 Exam Quick Recall Summary

### Provision

Present obligation + Probable outflow (>50%) + Reliable estimate.

### Contingent Liability

Possible obligation OR present obligation not probable/estimable. Disclose in notes.

### Contingent Asset

Ignore if possible; Board's report if probable; Asset in B/S if virtually certain.
