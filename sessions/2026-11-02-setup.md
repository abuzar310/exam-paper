## What was made

- **`render.py`**: turns an `answers.md` file into the PDF plus one PNG per page
  (A4, 300 DPI = 2481 × 3508 px). Arial, two columns, 4 mm margins.
- **The look was measured, not guessed.** From your pages: body text 8.5 pt on 10.25 pt
  lines, section titles 9.3 pt bold navy `#1F4E79` with a 1.6 pt rule, question lines
  7 pt bold blue with a thin grey rule, sub-headings 7.4 pt, tables 6.45 pt with a navy
  header row and light-blue every second row. Rendered on the same Letter page as your
  originals, every line breaks at the same word and sits within 1–2 px of the original.
- **One small improvement over the originals:** a heading never sits alone at the
  bottom of a column; it moves to the next column if fewer than 2 lines of its answer fit.
- **`samples/hrm-question-bank/`**: your 4 pages (`reference/`), the same answers typed
  out (`answers.md`), and the rebuilt PDF and PNGs (`output/`). It also works as HRM
  notes for later papers.
- **`README.md`** (the flow and the answer-file format) and **`CLAUDE.md`** (rules Claude
  follows here: notes first, internet last, 5 points for 5 marks, 10 for 10 marks,
  tables for differences).
- GitHub: **abuzar310/exam-paper** (private), branch `main`.

## Decisions

- **A4 by default** (what Indian print shops use). Your originals are Letter-size; use
  `--paper letter` for that. Same fonts and spacing either way; A4 fits a few more lines.
- No title or page numbers on the pages, same as your originals.

## Changed after setup (your instruction)

- **Question lines show the number only** (`Q1.`, `Q2.`), not the question text. You
  match each answer to the paper by its number. Same blue line and grey rule as before.
- **No sources mentioned**, not on the pages and not in chat. Answers still come from
  your notes first and the internet only when the notes don't cover a question.

- **Bigger text for 9-in-1 printing.** You printed the A4 pages 9 per sheet and the
  text was blurred and unreadable: each page shrinks to about a third, so 8.5 pt body
  text came out at 2.8 pt. Pages are now drawn 1.8× bigger with one column per page,
  so body text is about 5 pt on the printed sheet. A first try at 2.2× was dropped
  because you asked for "just readable", not more pages. The HRM sample went from 4
  pages (1 sheet, unreadable) to 14 pages (2 sheets, readable).
- Question numbers are now as big as the body text (8.5 pt bold) and table text went
  from 6.45 to 7.4 pt, because those were the hardest parts to read on the sheet.
- A question number is never left alone at the bottom of a page; the check now
  measures the first piece of the answer that can actually start there.
- **Back to two columns, 9 pages.** You said 1.8× was readable but too many pages
  ("9 pages before it was 4"), and that one column broke your rule: one sheet divided
  into two. Now: two columns, text 1.6× (about 4.2 pt on the 9-in-1 sheet), line
  spacing a little tighter (9.8 pt instead of 10.25), and margins and the column gap
  kept at their original size instead of growing with the text. The HRM answers with
  number-only question lines now fit 9 pages = exactly one 9-in-1 sheet. The original
  4 pages also took one sheet, but printed at 2.8 pt.
- Tables in the narrower columns: the first column widens to fit its longest word, so
  no word is split ("em ploye e" before).
- Fixed: a question number whose answer starts with a sub-heading ("On-the-job methods")
  could still be stranded at a column bottom.
- **Final choice: 10 pt answers, original layout.** You said the answers are what
  matter; question numbers and the 2/5/10 MARKS titles only need to be identifiable.
  Your original pages use 8.5 pt answers. Raising only the answers to 10 pt (line
  spacing and indent raised the same way, everything else at its original size) turns
  the 4 HRM pages into 5 on A4. The enlarging (1.6×) is off again. The 5 pages
  (`samples/hrm-question-bank/output/numbers-only-page-1..5.png`) were sent to you for a
  9-in-1 test print. On the sheet the answers come out at about 3.2 pt, between the
  2.7 pt that blurred and the 4.2 pt that read well.
