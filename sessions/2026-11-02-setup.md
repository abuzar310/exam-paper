## What you asked

- Solve question papers in the exact look of 4 HRM pages you had made before
  (2 MARKS / 5 MARKS / 10 MARKS, two columns), as a PDF and as high-quality images.
- The flow: you give notes and sources first, then the question paper. Answers come from
  your notes; only what the notes don't cover comes from the internet.
- Save the project as "exam paper", with the session history, on GitHub.

## What was made

- **`render.py`**: turns an `answers.md` file into the PDF plus one PNG per page
  (A4, 300 DPI = 2481 × 3508 px). Arial, two columns, 4 mm margins.
- **The look was measured, not guessed.** From your pages: body text 8.5 pt on 10.25 pt
  lines, section titles 9.3 pt bold navy `#1F4E79` with a 1.6 pt rule, question lines
  7 pt bold blue with a thin grey rule, sub-headings 7.4 pt, tables 6.45 pt with a navy
  header row and light-blue every second row. Rendered on the same Letter page as your
  originals, every line breaks at the same word and sits within 1–2 px of the original.
- **One small improvement over the originals:** a heading never sits alone at the
  bottom of a column; it moves to the next column if fewer than 2 lines of its answer fit.
- **`samples/hrm-question-bank/`**: your 4 pages (`reference/`), the same answers typed
  out (`answers.md`), and the rebuilt PDF and PNGs (`output/`). It also works as HRM
  notes for later papers.
- **`README.md`** (the flow and the answer-file format) and **`CLAUDE.md`** (rules Claude
  follows here: notes first, internet last, 5 points for 5 marks, 10 for 10 marks,
  tables for differences).
- GitHub: **abuzar310/exam-paper** (private), branch `main`.

## Decisions

- **A4 by default** (what Indian print shops use). Your originals are Letter-size; use
  `--paper letter` for that. Same fonts and spacing either way; A4 fits a few more lines.
- No title or page numbers on the pages, same as your originals.

## Changed after setup (your instruction)

- **Question lines show the number only** (`Q1.`, `Q2.`), not the question text. You
  match each answer to the paper by its number. Same blue line and grey rule as before.
- **No sources mentioned**, not on the pages and not in chat. Answers still come from
  your notes first and the internet only when the notes don't cover a question.
