# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Abuzar and his classmates in III B.Com at Don Bosco College, Chitradurga (Davangere University). The link is shared in the class WhatsApp group. They open it mostly in the daytime, on a phone at a print shop or on a laptop, to find a solved paper fast and print or share it.

## Product Purpose

One place for every solved question paper and every question paper of the semester, sorted by the seven subjects: Hindi, Corporate Accounting, English, Accounting Standards, Business Statistics, Human Resource Development, Personality Development. Each paper can be viewed or downloaded as a PDF, and its pages seen as images. Success is a student finding the right paper and printing it in under a minute.

## Positioning

The class's shelf of solved papers: every subject, ready to print.

## Brand Personality

Calm and focused, like a well-kept study desk. Plain words, no hype, no decoration that slows down finding a paper.

## Anti-references

Cluttered "notes download" sites full of ads, pop-ups and fake download buttons.

## Design Principles

- Finding beats browsing: subject, paper code and status are visible at a glance.
- Print is the end goal: the PDF is the main action, images are secondary.
- Honest emptiness: a subject with no papers says so plainly instead of hiding.
- Light on the phone: small thumbnails, no heavy scripts, works on a slow connection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable in daylight on a phone: strong contrast, large tap targets, real links and buttons that work with a keyboard, reduced motion respected.
