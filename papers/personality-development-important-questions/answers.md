# 2 MARKS — IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

## Q1. Define personality. [PYQ] [College]
**Personality** is the set of behaviours, thoughts, emotions and habits that make a person unique. It reflects how a person reacts, communicates, thinks and presents themselves in different situations, and it is shaped by heredity, environment and situation.

## Q2. What is procrastination? [PYQ]
**Procrastination** is the habit of delaying important tasks, often by doing easier or less important activities. It is caused by fear of failure, lack of motivation, feeling overwhelmed and distractions.

## Q3. Give the meaning of attitude. [PYQ]
**Attitude** is a mental state involving beliefs, feelings and behaviour towards a person, object or situation. In simple words, it is how we think and feel about something, which affects how we behave; it may be positive, negative or neutral.

## Q4. What is leadership? [PYQ]
**Leadership** is the ability to influence, guide and motivate others to achieve a common goal. A leader shows the way, supports the team and helps it succeed.

## Q5. What is conflict management? [PYQ]
**Conflict management** is the process of handling disagreements or arguments between individuals or groups in a peaceful and constructive way. It uses techniques such as compromising and collaborating to find a solution instead of blaming.

## Q6. What is communication? [PYQ]
**Communication** is the process of exchanging information, ideas, thoughts and feelings between people through words, symbols or actions. In simple words, it is how we talk, listen, write and understand each other.

## Q7. What is a group discussion? [PYQ]
**Group discussion (GD)** is a structured conversation in which participants express their ideas and opinions on a given topic within a time limit. It is used in placements, entrance exams and job selection to test communication, thinking and leadership skills.

## Q8. What is SWOT analysis?  [College]
**SWOT analysis** is a personal analysis tool that studies one's Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats. It helps a person understand themselves better and plan personal and career development.

## Q9. Mention two time management techniques.  [College]
1. **Pomodoro technique:** Study or work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.
2. **To-do list:** Prioritise tasks with a to-do list and follow a daily plan or schedule.

## Q10. What is self-monitoring?  [College]
**Self-monitoring** is the ability to observe and control one's own behaviour, emotions and appearance in different situations to fit social expectations. It means paying attention to how you act and how others react, and adjusting your behaviour if needed.

## Q11. Define perception.  [College]
**Perception** is the process of interpreting and understanding the information received through the senses. In simple words, it is how we see, understand and make sense of the world around us.

## Q12. Mention two types of attitude.  [College]
1. **Positive attitude:** Optimistic, hopeful and open-minded.
2. **Negative attitude:** Pessimistic, doubtful and resistant to change.

## Q13. What is assertive communication?  [College]
**Assertive communication** is expressing your thoughts, feelings and needs confidently and respectfully, without being aggressive or passive. It means standing up for yourself in a calm and honest way, using polite but firm language.

## Q14. List any two qualities of a good leader.  [College]
1. **Clear vision and strong communication:** The leader knows the goal and explains it clearly to the team.
2. **Integrity and honesty:** The leader is fair and truthful, so people trust and follow them.

## Q15. What is transactional analysis?  [College]
**Transactional analysis (TA)** is a psychological method, developed by Dr. Eric Berne, that studies interactions (transactions) between people based on their behaviour and communication. It explains these interactions through the Parent, Adult and Child ego states.

## Q16. Mention two symptoms of stress.  [College]
1. **Physical:** Headaches, tiredness and sleep problems.
2. **Emotional:** Anxiety, sadness and anger.

## Q17. Write two cyber etiquettes.  [College]
1. **Use polite language:** Be polite in emails, messages and posts, and avoid writing in all capital letters (it looks like shouting).
2. **Respect privacy:** Respect others' privacy online and do not spread fake news or offensive content.

## Q18. Write any two evaluated traits in a group discussion.  [College]
1. **Confidence:** Speaking up clearly and without fear.
2. **Active listening:** Listening to others and respecting their views.

## Q19. What is the STAR technique?  [College]
The **STAR technique** is a method of answering behavioural interview questions in four parts: Situation, Task, Action and Result. The candidate explains what happened, what had to be done, what they did and what the result was.

## Q20. What is a video resume?  [College]
A **video resume** is a short video in which a candidate introduces themselves and highlights their skills. It adds value to a job application and can be made with apps such as Canva, Biteable, InVideo or an unlisted YouTube video.

# 5 MARKS — IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

## Q1. What is self-awareness? Explain the benefits of self-awareness. [PYQ]
**Self-awareness** is the ability to understand your own personality, emotions, behaviour, strengths and weaknesses. Its benefits are:
1. **Realistic goals:** Knowing your strengths and weaknesses helps you set goals you can actually achieve.
2. **Better decisions and emotional control:** You understand your feelings, so you think before reacting and decide wisely.
3. **Stronger relationships:** Understanding yourself helps you understand others and get along well with them.
4. **Knowing how others see you:** It shows how your behaviour affects people, so you can improve your image.
5. **Self-improvement:** It helps you work on weak areas; for example, a person who gets nervous in public speaking can practise in small groups to build confidence.

## Q2. Explain the principles of goal setting. [PYQ]
1. **Specific:** The goal must be clear and well-defined, not vague.
2. **Measurable:** Progress must be trackable, so that you know how much has been achieved.
3. **Achievable:** The goal must be realistic and possible with the time and ability you have.
4. **Relevant:** It must match your personal or career aims, so that it is worth the effort.
5. **Time-bound:** It must have a fixed deadline. Example: "I will improve my spoken English by practising 30 minutes daily for 3 months."

## Q3. What are the factors influencing perception? Explain. [PYQ]
1. **Attitude and values of the perceiver:** A person sees things through his own beliefs and values, so two people may judge the same event differently.
2. **Past experience:** What a person has experienced before shapes how he interprets a new person or situation.
3. **Interests, needs and expectations:** People notice what interests them or what they expect to see, and ignore the rest.
4. **Situational factors:** The time, place and environment in which something is seen affect how it is understood; a loud voice at a party and in an office are perceived differently.
5. **Target factors:** The features of the person or object observed, such as appearance, behaviour, novelty and size, influence the perception formed.

## Q4. Explain the barriers to attitude change. [PYQ]
**Attitude** is a mental state involving beliefs, feelings and behaviour towards a person, object or situation; some factors make it hard to change.
1. **Strong beliefs or habits:** Beliefs and habits built over many years are held firmly, so new ideas are resisted.
2. **Peer pressure:** Friends and group members may discourage a person from changing his views.
3. **Fear of change:** People feel safe with what is familiar and fear the unknown.
4. **Lack of awareness:** A person may not know the new facts or see the need to change.
5. **Lack of motivation:** Without a reason or reward, a person sees no benefit in changing.

## Q5. Explain the qualities of an effective leader. [PYQ]
1. **Clear vision and strong communication:** An effective leader knows the goal and explains it clearly to the team.
2. **Confidence and decision-making ability:** He takes firm and timely decisions and faces problems with confidence.
3. **Empathy and emotional intelligence:** He understands the feelings of team members and controls his own emotions.
4. **Integrity and honesty:** He is fair and truthful and keeps his word, so people trust and follow him.
5. **Ability to motivate and support others:** He encourages the team, helps members grow and gets the best out of them.

## Q6. What are the different types of conflicts? Explain with examples. [PYQ] [College]
1. **Meaning:** A conflict is a disagreement or argument between individuals or groups; it is handled in a peaceful and constructive way through conflict management.
2. **Intrapersonal conflict:** Conflict within oneself. Example: confusion about choosing a career.
3. **Interpersonal conflict:** Conflict between two people. Example: a disagreement between classmates.
4. **Intragroup conflict:** Conflict within a group or team. Example: team members not agreeing on a plan.
5. **Intergroup conflict:** Conflict between two groups. Example: two departments arguing over project control.

## Q7. Explain the process of communication. [PYQ]
**Communication** is the process of exchanging information, ideas, thoughts and feelings between people. Its process is: sender → message → medium → receiver → feedback.
1. **Sender:** The person who starts the communication and puts the idea into words, symbols or gestures (encoding).
2. **Message:** The information, idea or feeling that is to be shared.
3. **Medium or channel:** The method used to send the message, such as spoken, written or digital.
4. **Receiver:** The person who receives the message and understands its meaning (decoding).
5. **Feedback and noise:** Feedback is the receiver's response, which completes the process; noise is any physical or emotional disturbance that spoils the message.

## Q8. Explain the types of interviews. [PYQ]
1. **Personal interview:** A one-on-one discussion between the candidate and one interviewer.
2. **Panel interview:** Several interviewers question one candidate together, each judging a different area.
3. **Telephonic / video interview:** The interview is conducted remotely by phone or video call, often as a first round.
4. **Group interview:** Several candidates are interviewed together to see how they behave and communicate in a group.
5. **Behavioural interview:** It focuses on the candidate's past experiences and actions, and is best answered with the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

## Q9. Explain the determinants of personality with examples.  [College]
1. **Meaning:** Personality is the set of behaviours, thoughts, emotions and habits that make a person unique; it is shaped by heredity, environment and situation.
2. **Heredity:** Traits inherited from parents, such as body structure, intelligence and temperament. Example: a child may have the calm temperament of a parent.
3. **Family environment:** Upbringing, values and habits learnt at home shape how a person thinks and behaves.
4. **Social and cultural environment:** School, peer groups, media and culture influence a person's attitudes, language and behaviour. Example: a student in an active college club grows more confident.
5. **Situation:** Behaviour may change with the situation. Example: someone may be quiet at home but confident in a classroom or office.

## Q10. Describe the benefits of self-awareness and how to develop it.  [College]
**Self-awareness** is the ability to understand your own personality, emotions, behaviour, strengths and weaknesses.
1. **Realistic goals and better decisions:** Knowing your strengths and weaknesses helps you set achievable goals and improves decision-making and emotional control.
2. **Stronger relationships:** It builds better relationships and helps you understand how others perceive you.
3. **Self-reflection and a journal:** Think about your own actions and keep a personal diary or journal.
4. **Feedback from others:** Ask teachers, friends and colleagues for honest feedback and act on it.
5. **Personality assessment tools:** Use tools such as MBTI or the Johari Window. Example: if you get nervous during public speaking, practise in small groups to build confidence.

## Q11. Explain the importance of goal setting and principles of SMART goals.  [College]
**Goal setting** is the process of identifying something you want to achieve and making a plan to reach it.
1. **Importance:** It gives clarity and direction, increases motivation and focus, helps measure progress and encourages discipline and planning.
2. **Specific and Measurable:** The goal must be clear and well-defined, and its progress must be trackable.
3. **Achievable:** The goal must be realistic and possible with the time and ability you have.
4. **Relevant and Time-bound:** It must match your personal or career goals and have a fixed deadline.
5. **Example and tips:** "I will improve my spoken English by practising 30 minutes daily for 3 months." Write goals down, break them into small steps and review progress regularly.

## Q12. What are perceptual errors? Explain any three with examples.  [College]
**Perceptual errors** are mistakes we make while judging people or situations, because we interpret information wrongly.
1. **Stereotyping:** Judging someone based on their group identity. Example: thinking all teenagers are lazy.
2. **Halo effect:** Forming a whole opinion from one good quality. Example: thinking someone is intelligent just because they speak well.
3. **Horn effect:** Judging someone negatively because of one bad quality. Example: thinking a late-comer is careless in all work.
4. **Other errors:** First impression error (quick judgement without the full picture) and projection (assuming others think and feel as you do).
5. **Avoiding errors:** Be aware of your own biases, avoid jumping to conclusions, collect more information before judging and keep an open mind.

## Q14. Explain the importance of team building and types of teams.  [College]
**Team building** is the process of creating a group of individuals who work together effectively to achieve common goals.
1. **Importance:** It encourages cooperation and trust, improves communication, increases productivity and creativity, reduces conflicts and builds a sense of belonging.
2. **Formal teams:** Created by an organisation with a clear structure and goals. Example: a project team in a company.
3. **Informal teams:** Formed naturally by people with common interests. Example: friends working together on a college event.
4. **Cross-functional teams:** Members from different departments. Example: marketing, finance and HR working together.
5. **Virtual teams:** Teams that work online or remotely. Example: a remote customer support team.

## Q15. Describe the leadership styles and qualities of a 21st-century leader.  [College]
1. **Autocratic and democratic:** An autocratic leader takes decisions alone (military); a democratic leader involves the team in decisions (a project leader who takes suggestions).
2. **Laissez-faire:** The leader gives freedom to team members. Example: creative industries such as advertising or design.
3. **Transformational and transactional:** A transformational leader inspires and brings change; a transactional leader rewards or punishes based on performance (a sales manager giving bonuses for targets).
4. **Qualities of an effective leader:** Clear vision and strong communication, confidence and decision-making, empathy and emotional intelligence, integrity and honesty, and the ability to motivate others.
5. **21st-century competencies:** Agility (quick, flexible decisions), empathy (understanding others' feelings), adaptability (adjusting to change and uncertainty) and technological awareness (using digital tools to lead).

## Q16. Define negotiation. Explain its process with an example.  [College]
**Negotiation** is the process where two or more people discuss and reach an agreement that benefits all parties. Example: a college fest committee hiring a sound system.
1. **Preparation:** Know your goals, the facts and the other party's needs. The committee fixes its budget at Rs. 15,000 and checks other vendors' rates.
2. **Opening:** Start the discussion politely and professionally. The committee meets the vendor and explains the event.
3. **Discussion:** Share ideas, offers and concerns, and listen actively. The vendor quotes Rs. 20,000 and explains the cost of the equipment.
4. **Bargaining:** Make offers and find compromises for a win-win result. The committee offers Rs. 16,000 plus the vendor's name on the fest banner.
5. **Closing and implementation:** Agree on the final terms and follow through. Both agree on Rs. 16,500 with the banner, put it in writing and keep their word on the day.

## Q17. Discuss the Johari Window model and its relevance to communication.  [College]
The **Johari Window** is a model to improve self-awareness and communication, which divides what is known about a person into four areas.
1. **Open area:** Known to self and to others.
2. **Hidden area:** Known to self but not to others.
3. **Blind spot:** Unknown to self but known to others.
4. **Unknown area:** Unknown to self and to others.
5. **Relevance to communication:** Sharing about yourself reduces the hidden area and asking for feedback reduces the blind spot, so the open area grows, which leads to open and honest communication, trust and fewer misunderstandings.

## Q18. What is emotional intelligence? Explain its components.  [College]
**Emotional intelligence (EI)** is the ability to understand, manage and express your own emotions, and to recognise and influence the emotions of others.
1. **Self-awareness:** Knowing your own emotions.
2. **Self-regulation:** Controlling your emotions and pausing before reacting.
3. **Motivation:** Staying positive and driven.
4. **Empathy:** Understanding others' feelings.
5. **Social skills:** Communicating and managing relationships. Example: a team leader who stays calm in a crisis and motivates the team.

## Q19. Explain how to manage stress using practical techniques.  [College]
**Stress** is the physical or emotional pressure a person feels when overwhelmed by demands or challenges.
1. **Time management:** Plan your schedule and avoid last-minute work.
2. **Exercise and a healthy lifestyle:** Exercise releases stress and improves mood; eat well, sleep well and avoid harmful habits.
3. **Relaxation techniques:** Deep breathing, meditation and yoga.
4. **Talk to someone:** Share your problems with a friend, family member or counsellor.
5. **Positive thinking:** Focus on solutions, not just problems. Example: for exam stress, make a study timetable and take short breaks.

## Q20. Explain table manners and the do's and don'ts of formal dining.  [College]
**Table manners** are the polite behaviours followed while eating, especially in formal or multicultural settings.
1. **Importance:** They show respect and discipline, reflect one's upbringing and personality, and help during business lunches or interviews.
2. **Do's – sitting and starting:** Sit upright, avoid placing elbows on the table and wait for everyone before starting the meal.
3. **Do's – while eating:** Use cutlery properly, chew with your mouth closed and say "excuse me" politely if you need to leave the table.
4. **Don'ts:** Don't talk with food in your mouth, don't make noise while eating (slurping or loud chewing) and don't use your phone at the table.
5. **Multicultural awareness:** Different cultures have different eating habits; understanding and respecting them is important in global workplaces.

## Q21. Discuss professional dress code and grooming tips for interviews.  [College]
A **dress code** is a set of rules about what clothing is suitable for different occasions, especially in professional environments.
1. **Formal wear:** Formal wear is used for interviews, presentations and corporate events; clothes must be clean and ironed.
2. **Dress for men and women:** Formal shirts, trousers and shoes for men; formal suits, kurtis or sarees for women.
3. **Accessories:** Wear minimal accessories and light perfume.
4. **Personal grooming:** Take regular baths, use deodorant, and keep nails clean and trimmed and hair neat.
5. **Neat finish:** Avoid excessive make-up or jewellery, and wear polished shoes and well-fitted clothes.

## Q22. What is group discussion? Write the process and key evaluation criteria.  [College]
A **group discussion (GD)** is a structured conversation in which participants express their ideas and opinions on a given topic within a time limit.
1. **Topic and thinking time:** The topic is given (current affairs, abstract or case-based) and participants get time to think.
2. **Discussion:** The discussion begins and each member contributes, without interrupting others.
3. **Conclusion and feedback:** The group may try to reach a conclusion, and observers or evaluators give feedback.
4. **Evaluation – content and communication:** The quality of ideas, and clear and confident speaking.
5. **Evaluation – team, body language and time:** Team coordination, body language and time management.

## Q23. Describe five essential interview skills for job success.  [College]
1. **Communication and body language:** Speak clearly, maintain eye contact and keep a good posture.
2. **Listening and understanding questions:** Listen carefully and answer exactly what is asked.
3. **Clarity of thought and confidence:** Give logical, honest answers with confidence.
4. **Knowledge of the subject and company:** Know your subject and research the company and the job role.
5. **Planning and professional conduct:** Prepare answers to common questions, practise mock interviews, dress properly and be punctual.
