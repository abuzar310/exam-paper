# Personality Development: question bank

B.Com. 3rd semester examination question bank, Personality Development (course code B.Com. 3.5), prepared by Daneial D, Assistant Professor. Photos: `question-paper-1.jpg` to `question-paper-3.jpg`. The same bank is in `notes/personality-development/question-bank.pdf`.

## Section A: 2-mark questions (answer in 2-3 sentences)

Module 1: Personality, self-awareness, SWOT and goal setting
1. Define Personality.
2. What is SWOT Analysis?
3. Define Self-Awareness.
4. Mention two time management techniques.
5. What is Procrastination?

Module 2: Self-monitoring, perception, attitude and assertiveness
6. What is Self-Monitoring?
7. Define Perception.
8. Mention two types of Attitude.
9. What is Assertive Communication?
10. Differentiate briefly between passive and aggressive behaviour.

Module 3: Team building, leadership, negotiation and conflict management
11. List any two qualities of a good leader.
12. Define Negotiation.
13. What is Conflict Management?
14. Mention the different levels of conflict.

Module 4: Communication, EI, stress and transactional analysis
15. What is Transactional Analysis (TA)?
16. Name the four Life Positions in Transactional Analysis.
17. What is Emotional Intelligence (EI)?
18. Mention two symptoms of stress.

Module 5: Social graces, group discussion and interview skills
19. What is a Video Resume?
20. State two Cyber Etiquettes.
21. What is the STAR technique in interviews?
22. Write any two evaluated traits in a Group Discussion (GD).
