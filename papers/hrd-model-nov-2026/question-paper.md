# Human Resource Development: model examination, November 2026

Don Bosco College, Chitradurga (affiliated to Davangere University). II B.Com. Date 12/11/2026, 9:30 to 12:30 pm, 180 minutes, 80 marks. Photo: `question-paper.jpg`.

## Section A: Answer any TEN questions (10 × 2 = 16 marks)

1. Define Human Resource Development (HRD).
2. What is potential appraisal?
3. What is an assessment centre?
4. Mention any two HRD mechanisms.
5. What is organisational climate?
6. What is meant by a learning style?
7. What is human resource accounting?
8. What is an HR audit?
9. State any two differences between training and development.
10. What is a balanced scorecard?
11. What is Aptitude, Knowledge and Values?
12. What is Human Resource Planning?

## Section B: Answer any FIVE questions (6 × 5 = 30 marks)

1. Trace the evolution of HRD from personnel management.
2. Explain the subsystems of HRD.
3. Explain the external and internal factors influencing employee behaviour.
4. Explain the principles of learning and how learning can be maximised.
5. Explain the process of training need analysis.
6. Write short notes on (a) outbound training and (b) attitudinal training.
7. Discuss the recent trends in HRD, with special reference to competency mapping.

## Section C: Answer any FOUR questions (3 × 10 = 30 marks)

1. Define HRD. Explain HRD at the macro and micro levels, and the qualities and competencies required of an HRD professional.
2. Explain the HRD mechanisms. Discuss the role of organisational climate and culture in HRD.
3. Explain the concept of developing human capacity with reference to aptitude, knowledge, values and skills of human relations. Discuss leadership development.
4. Explain HR accounting, HR audit and benchmarking as tools for evaluating HRD, and discuss the impact of HRD initiatives on the bottom line of an organisation.
5. Explain the principles involved in the selection of a training method and the techniques of training at different levels. How is training effectiveness evaluated?
