# SECTION C — answer any three (3 × 10 = 30)

**10.** *(M1; Dec 2025 type)* A company issued 1,00,000 equity shares. The issue was underwritten A 40%, B 35%, C 25%, with firm underwriting of A 10,000, B 5,000 and C 5,000 shares. Total applications received (including firm underwriting) were 85,000 shares. Marked applications: A 25,000, B 22,000, C 13,000. Determine the total liability of each underwriter (firm underwriting shares credited to the underwriter concerned).

**11.** *(M2; Dec 2025 type)* Sunrise Ltd. was incorporated on 1.7.2023 to take over a business from 1.4.2023; accounts closed on 31.3.2024. Gross profit for the year was Rs. 5,00,000. Sales were Rs. 2,00,000 before incorporation and Rs. 8,00,000 after. Other items for the year: Salaries Rs. 48,000; Rent and taxes Rs. 24,000; Office expenses Rs. 12,000; Commission on sales Rs. 20,000; Advertisement Rs. 10,000; Carriage outward Rs. 5,000; Bad debts Rs. 5,000; Director's fees Rs. 6,000; Preliminary expenses written off Rs. 8,000; Interest on debentures Rs. 12,000. Ascertain the profit prior to and after incorporation.

**12.** *(M3; Dec 2025 type)* From the following, calculate the value of one equity share under (a) the net asset method, (b) the yield method and (c) the fair value method. 20,000 8% preference shares of Rs. 10 each, fully paid; 50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10 each, fully paid; external liabilities Rs. 2,00,000; total assets Rs. 12,00,000, including fictitious assets Rs. 40,000; average profit after tax Rs. 1,45,000; transfer to general reserve 20% of profit; normal rate of return 10%.

**13.** *(M4; Dec 2025 type)* From the following balances of Rohit Ltd. as on 31.3.2025, prepare the Balance Sheet as per Schedule III. Equity share capital (Rs. 10 each) Rs. 10,00,000; Securities premium Rs. 2,00,000; General reserve Rs. 1,50,000; Surplus (P&L) Rs. 40,000; 10% Debentures Rs. 5,00,000; Trade payables Rs. 2,50,000; Provision for tax Rs. 50,000; Proposed dividend Rs. 60,000; Land and building Rs. 8,00,000; Plant and machinery Rs. 6,00,000; Furniture Rs. 1,00,000; Goodwill Rs. 1,50,000; Non-current investments Rs. 2,00,000; Inventories Rs. 2,00,000; Trade receivables Rs. 1,50,000; Cash and bank Rs. 50,000.

**14.** *(M5; Gap — liquidator's final statement)* Prepare the Liquidator's Final Statement of Account of a company. Assets realised Rs. 9,00,000.
