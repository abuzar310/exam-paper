1. Define Accounting Standards.
2. State any two objectives of setting Accounting Standards.
3. What is Net Realisable Value (NRV) in inventory valuation as per AS-2?
4. What constitutes the cost of an item of Property, Plant, and Equipment under AS-10?
5. Differentiate between Current Investments and Long-term Investments according to AS-13.
6. Define "Qualifying Asset" as per AS-16 (Borrowing Costs).
7. Distinguish between a Finance Lease and an Operating Lease under AS-19.

## Section B — 5 marks each. Answer any four. (4 × 5 = 20)

1. Explain the standard-setting process in India by the Accounting Standards Board (ASB) and the National Financial Reporting Authority (NFRA).
2. ABC Ltd. purchased machinery from a foreign supplier for Rs. 20,00,000. It incurred the following expenses:
   - Non-refundable import duty: Rs. 2,00,000
   - Refundable GST (input tax credit available): Rs. 2,40,000
   - Freight and handling charges: Rs. 80,000
   - Installation and site preparation cost: Rs. 1,20,000
   - Inauguration event expenditure: Rs. 50,000
   - Initial operating losses before full production capacity: Rs. 1,00,000
   - Staff training costs: Rs. 40,000

   Calculate the cost at which the machinery should be capitalised as per AS 10.
3. A company has historical cost of inventory as Rs. 5,00,000. Due to market fluctuations, the estimated selling price of the inventory is Rs. 4,80,000, and selling expenses are estimated at 5% of the selling price. Calculate the value at which the inventory should be shown in the financial statements as per AS-2.
4. ABC Ltd. purchased a machine for Rs. 20,00,000. It paid import duties of Rs. 2,00,000, non-refundable taxes of Rs. 1,00,000, installation charges of Rs. 1,50,000, and site preparation costs of Rs. 50,000. It also spent Rs. 30,000 on an inauguration event for the new machinery. Calculate the initial cost of the machine to be capitalised as per AS-10.
5. X Ltd. borrowed Rs. 50,00,000 at an interest rate of 10% per annum on 1st April 2024 to construct a factory building (a qualifying asset). The construction commenced on 1st April 2024 and was completed on 31st December 2024. During this period, unutilised funds were temporarily invested, earning interest income of Rs. 50,000. Calculate the amount of borrowing costs to be capitalised as per AS-16.
6. A company is involved in a lawsuit filed by a customer claiming damages of Rs. 10,00,000 for a defective supply. As of 31st March 2025, the company's legal counsel advises that it is probable (75% chance) that the company will lose the lawsuit and will have to pay Rs. 6,00,000. How should the company treat this situation in its financial statements as per AS-29?

## Section C — 10 marks each. Answer all. The sheet prints 3 × 10 = 30, and five questions follow that heading.

1. SkyWays Airlines purchased an aircraft on 1 April 2020 for Rs. 100 crore. The body of the aircraft costs Rs. 70 crore (useful life 20 years), and its main engine costs Rs. 30 crore (useful life 5 years, straight-line method). On 31 March 2025 (after 5 years), the old engine is replaced with a new advanced engine costing Rs. 45 crore. Explain the accounting treatment as per AS 10.
2. Lessor Ltd. leased a machine to Lessee Ltd. on 1 April 2022 for 4 years. Fair value Rs. 10,00,000. Annual lease rent at the end of each year Rs. 3,20,000. Guaranteed residual value by the lessee Rs. 50,000. Unguaranteed residual value Rs. 30,000. Implicit rate 10% per annum. PV factors at 10% for years 1, 2, 3 and 4: 0.909, 0.826, 0.751, 0.683. Calculate the present value of minimum lease payments. State whether the lease is a finance lease or an operating lease as per AS 19.
3. Explain Accounting Standard 9 in detail.
4. On 1 April 2024, Surya Ltd. purchased 5,000, 10% debentures of Rs. 100 each in Zenith Ltd. at Rs. 96 per debenture cum-interest. Interest is payable half-yearly, on 30 June and 31 December. Brokerage is 1% of the transaction value excluding interest. Stamp duty and transfer fees Rs. 2,000. On 1 November 2024, Surya Ltd. sold 2,000 of these debentures at Rs. 98 per debenture ex-interest. Brokerage of 1% was paid on the sale. On 31 March 2025 the market price was Rs. 92 per debenture. The debentures are current investments. Find the cost on 1 April 2024, the profit or loss on the sale, the interest income up to 31 March 2025, the year-end valuation, and prepare the Investment in 10% Debentures Account.
5. XYZ Construction Ltd. undertakes an industrial complex. Separate proposals were raised for each unit. Each unit was subject to separate negotiation, and the costs and revenues of each unit can be identified. Should each unit be treated as a separate construction contract under AS 7?
