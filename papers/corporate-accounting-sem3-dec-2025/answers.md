# SECTION A - 2 MARKS

## Q1. (a)
**Valuation of shares** is the process of finding the real worth of a company's share. It is done when shares are not quoted, or when the market price does not show the true value, for example on amalgamation, conversion of shares, or a purchase of a block of shares.

## Q1. (b)
**Goodwill** is the extra earning power of a business over and above the normal return on the capital employed. It is an intangible asset that arises from reputation, location, efficient management and customer connections.

## Q1. (c)
A **liquidator** (Company Liquidator) is the person appointed to wind up a company. He realises the assets, pays the costs and the creditors in the legal order, and distributes any surplus to the shareholders.

## Q1. (d)
Preferential creditors are paid before the unsecured creditors. Under Sec. 327 of the Companies Act, 2013, four of them are:
1. **Government dues:** taxes, cesses and rates that became due and payable within 12 months before winding up.
2. **Wages and salary** of employees for not more than 4 months, within the prescribed limit.
3. **Accrued holiday remuneration** payable to employees.
4. **Provident fund, pension, gratuity and ESI** contributions payable by the company as employer.

## Q1. (e)
**Profit prior to incorporation** is the profit earned from the date a running business is taken over to the date the company is incorporated. The company has no legal existence in that period, so this profit is a capital profit. It is transferred to capital reserve and cannot be paid as dividend.

## Q1. (f)
1. **Intangible:** Goodwill has no physical form.
2. **Not separable:** It can be sold only along with the business, not by itself.
3. **Fluctuating value:** Its value changes with the earning capacity of the business and does not fall merely by use.
4. **Helps to earn super profit:** It is the reason the business earns more than the normal return.

## Q1. (g)
In the Statement of Profit and Loss (Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013):
1. **Bad debts** are recorded under **Other expenses**.
2. **Employer's contribution to provident fund** is recorded under **Employee benefits expense**.

## Q1. (h)
**Underwriting of shares** is a contract in which underwriters agree, for a commission, to take up the shares that the public does not subscribe. It assures the company that the whole issue will be taken up.

## Q1. (i)
**Firm underwriting** is a definite commitment by an underwriter to take a fixed number of shares, whether or not the public subscribes for them. Those shares are his liability in addition to his share of any shortfall in the public issue.

## Q1. (j)
**Super profit** is the excess of the average profit over the normal profit. Normal profit is capital employed multiplied by the normal rate of return. Super profit = Average profit minus Normal profit.

## Q10.
These are the methods named in Davangere University B.Com. 3.1 (SEP). The prescribed books are Maheshwari, Jain and Narang, Shukla and Grewal, and Tulsian.

### Valuation of shares
1. **Net assets method (intrinsic value).** The share is worth the net assets that belong to it. Real assets are taken, fictitious assets such as preliminary expenses are excluded, and outside liabilities and preference capital are deducted. Intrinsic value per equity share = Net assets available to equity holders / Number of equity shares.
2. **Yield method.** The share is worth what its dividend, or the earnings on it, yields as compared with the normal rate. Expected rate = (Profit available for equity / Equity paid-up capital) x 100. Yield value = (Expected rate / Normal rate) x Paid-up value of one share.
3. **Fair value method.** This is the simple average of the other two, and it is used when both the asset backing and the yield should be recognised. Fair value = (Intrinsic value + Yield value) / 2.

### Valuation of goodwill
1. **Average profits method.** Average profit = Total of the adjusted profits / Number of years. Goodwill = Average profit x Number of years' purchase. Abnormal items are removed before the average is taken.
2. **Super profits method.** Normal profit = Capital employed x Normal rate of return. Super profit = Average profit minus Normal profit. Goodwill = Super profit x Number of years' purchase. Goodwill arises only when the business earns more than the normal return.
3. **Annuity method.** Super profit is treated as an annuity for a fixed number of years, and it is discounted. Goodwill = Super profit x Present value of an annuity of Re. 1 for the given years at the normal rate. The goodwill is lower than under a plain years' purchase because later years' profit is discounted.
